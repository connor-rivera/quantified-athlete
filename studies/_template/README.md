# NN: Study title

**Status:** Planned / Pre-registered / In progress / Complete

## Question
One or two sentences: what are you trying to find out, and why does it matter?

## Pre-registration
Link to `preregistration.md` if the study was planned before looking at the data, and when it was committed. Otherwise say "Exploratory, not pre-registered."

## Results

## Limitations
